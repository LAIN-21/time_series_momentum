# Short — Momentum as a conviction dial

Instead of only 12m, average the signs of 1m + 3m + 6m + 12m.

Score = +1 when all agree long, −1 when all agree short, near 0 when they fight.

That’s not magic alpha — it’s **position sizing from agreement**.

On SPY alone, Sharpe didn’t beat plain 12m (higher turnover tax). The idea still matters once you diversify.
